Bank Account Control

Choose which accounts users can pay into. Deactivate an account that is nearing its limit and activate another one — users instantly see only the active accounts, on every device. At least one account must always stay active.

Scan User Withdrawals

Scans every withdrawal you have approved and permanently debits it from that user — the money moves out of their Pending balance into Withdrawn, so it can never be withdrawn twice. Run this after paying users, or use it to clean up old approved requests where the debit never went through. Safe to re-run: already-debited requests are skipped automatically.

Reset Referral Commissions

Two-phase repair. Phase 1 credits commissions on approved payments that were never flagged as paid. Phase 2 audits every referrer's referral count against the members who truly activated a package, and back-credits commissions that were approved earlier but never arrived — this catches records damaged by the old approval bug. Commissions already received are detected automatically, so nothing is ever credited twice. A commission is only due after the referred member's payment is approved, and the referrer must have an active package (earning lock). Safe to re-run.

Scan & Verify User Balances

History audit (never resets balances): checks every user against their raw history — tasks completed, referral commissions earned, and every withdrawal ever placed (pending, approved, paid, rejected) — works out exactly what their balance should be, and corrects only the difference. Excess money left behind by old bugs is debited, missing money is credited, and every balance that already matches its history is left completely untouched. Safe to re-run: clean users are skipped automatically.

Fund a User (Manual Credit)

Enter a user's phone number to find them, then credit their task balance with any amount. The money is added to both their Task Earnings and Available balance, visible on their dashboard instantly.
